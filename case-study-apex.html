KNOWLEDGE COMMERCE & VIDEO COACHING

Apex Digital Academy

Engineering a premier cohort-based educational platform and frictionless 4-step sales funnel integrating Kajabi, WordPress LMS, and Stripe Elements to maximize student checkout conversion.

Client Industry
Knowledge Commerce & EdTech
Services Provided
Funnel Architecture, Kajabi / WordPress Integration, Checkout UX
Key Stack
Kajabi, WordPress LMS, Stripe Elements, Adaptive Video CDN
Conversion Metric
8.4% Funnel Conversion Rate

Project Overview

Apex Digital Academy provides high-ticket cohort learning and certification for engineering professionals. Despite strong word-of-mouth reputation, prospective students were abandoning checkout flows due to sluggish video players and disjointed login screens across separate subdomains.

The Challenge

The existing checkout process required multiple redirects between marketing landing pages, payment gateways, and student dashboards. This cognitive friction severely depressed conversion rates for $1,500+ cohort programs.

The Approach

We consolidated their marketing and learning infrastructure into a unified brand experience. We introduced an integrated 4-step funnel that allowed prospective students to preview curriculum lessons before completing a single-screen checkout with Apple Pay and Google Pay support.

Key Architectural Features

Frictionless one-page checkout powered by Stripe Elements and native digital wallet detection.
Adaptive bitrate video delivery via global edge CDN, guaranteeing instant playback on all network speeds.
Custom student progress telemetry dashboard with automated milestone certificate generation.
Automated onboarding webhook triggers routing new students directly to cohort community channels.

Results & Business Outcomes

The unified funnel lifted total checkout completion to 8.4% (quadruple the EdTech industry benchmark of 2.1%), supporting the successful onboarding of over 12,800 active paying students within the platform's first year.
