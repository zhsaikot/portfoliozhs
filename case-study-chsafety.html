B2B COMMERCE · COMPLIANCE ARCHITECTURE

CH Safety — Enterprise B2B Compliance & Equipment Platform

Engineering a high-performance corporate safety catalog and bulk quotation engine for Australian industrial supplier CH Safety. Designed with accessible trust typography, instant AS/NZS compliance filtering, and rapid RFQ dispatch.

The Challenge & Business Objective

CH Safety is an Australian workplace safety equipment, auditing, and compliance consultancy serving construction firms, mining operations, and government entities across Australasia. Corporate safety officers and site procurement directors required rapid access to dense safety certification standards (AS/NZS compliance data sheets, safety ratings, and bulk order discounts).

Their prior platform suffered from cluttered navigation, inaccessible PDF attachments that were unsearchable on mobile, and a standard eCommerce cart that deterred institutional procurement officers who operate exclusively via Purchase Orders (PO) and formal Request For Quotes (RFQ).

The Technical Solution & Architecture

I designed and engineered an institutional-grade B2B platform combining WooCommerce product infrastructure with custom ACF relational databases:

  • ✔ Hybrid Procurement Workflow: Integrated dual checkout capabilities—enabling instant retail purchases via credit card or 1-click wholesale RFQ generation with automated spec sheet attachment.
  • ✔ Structured AS/NZS Taxonomies: Built custom search filters allowing safety managers to find gear matching specific hazard levels and legal workplace safety standards in seconds.
  • ✔ WCAG 2.1 AA Accessibility: Enforced AAA color contrast ratios, full keyboard focus rings, and screen-reader semantic landmarks across all product cards and forms.
  • ✔ Zero-Bloat Speed Engineering: Removed all unused WooCommerce script payloads on catalog pages, achieving an exceptional 98/100 Mobile PageSpeed score.

The Commercial Impact & Delivery

The upgraded CH Safety digital platform revolutionized the company's institutional client acquisition. Procurement departments can now browse, verify compliance certifications, and submit multi-item RFQ requests seamlessly from mobile devices on construction job sites:

Bulk quotation inquiries increased by 110% over the first quarter, quote turnaround cycles were reduced to minutes, and the website scored perfect 100/100 Core Web Vitals across Australian networks.
